Who ends up within reach

Share of each group living inside the service radius, counting the existing centres plus the new ones. The marker is the figure for all residents, so a bar short of it means that group is behind.

What one more cooling centre buys

Coverage can only rise as centres are added, and the exact optimum sits at or above the greedy solution everywhere. The useful quantity is the slope: how much reach the next centre adds. Realistic budgets stop around twenty, well before the curve flattens.

What goes with heat vulnerability, once the heat itself is held fixed

This describes relationships; it does not predict anything. Surface temperature, pavement, canopy and density enter as controls, and the social variables are the ones being read. The question is which populations end up most vulnerable among places that are physically alike. Folds are spatial, so neighbouring tracts cannot leak between training and test.
