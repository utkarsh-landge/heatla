How much does one more cooling centre buy?

Every curve is monotonic — more centres never means less coverage — and the ILP is always at or above greedy. The slope is the policy number: it is the marginal benefit of the next centre. Realistic budgets are around 10–20 sites, which is still on the steep part of the curve.

What is associated with heat vulnerability, net of physical heat?

This is an association study, not a deployed predictor. Physical heat variables (surface temperature, pavement, canopy, density) enter as controls; the social variables are the ones of interest. The question is: among places that are physically similar, which populations end up most vulnerable? Cross-validation uses spatial k-means folds, and the R² is a check that the fitted surface is stable enough to read — not a result.
